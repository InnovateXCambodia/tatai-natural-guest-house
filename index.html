A Real Tatai Stay

River life, mountain views and simple Khmer hospitality.

Tatai Natural Guest House is a small family-run place made for guests who want to be close to the water, nature and everyday local life.

  • Along the Tatai River The guest house sits directly on the river, facing the Cardamom Mountains.
  • Brackish & tidal water Freshwater flowing from the Tatai waterfalls mixes with saltwater connected to the sea, so the river rises and falls with the tide.
  • White-sand riverbed A distinctive natural feature of the river around the guest house.
  • Swim straight from the bungalow deck Guests have direct river access for swimming right from the property.
  • Fishing onsite You can fish around the guest house without booking a separate tour.
  • Cardamom Mountain views Forest-covered mountains form the backdrop across the river.
Good to know

There is no air-conditioning. Rooms use fans and natural ventilation, so this stay is best for guests who enjoy simple comfort and being genuinely close to nature.

River Life

Swim, paddle,fish — or simplysit and watchthe river.

Guests can swim directly from the property. The Tatai River here is tidal and brackish, with freshwater flowing from the surrounding landscape and a white-sand riverbed in the area.

Things to Do

The river is already part of your stay.

You do not need to book a tour to enjoy Tatai here. Swim from the property, take a kayak out on the river, or spend time fishing. When you want to explore farther, the family can arrange off-site trips around Koh Kong.

TATAI WATERFALL

See Tatai at its most dramatic.

Tatai Waterfall is one of Koh Kong’s best-known natural attractions. Its broad rocky cascades change with the season — calmer in the dry months and far more powerful during the rains.

Ask about the Waterfall Trip →
CARDAMOM MOUNTAINS

Rainforest rising behind the river.

Koh Kong is where the Cardamom Mountains meet Cambodia’s coast. The forested range forms the green backdrop you see across the Tatai River and is one of the defining landscapes of southwest Cambodia.

Ask about a Cardamom trip →
KOH SRALAO

A living fishing community.

Koh Sralao is a small fishing island community in Koh Kong’s Peam Krasop Wildlife Sanctuary, home to hundreds of fishing families and closely tied to the surrounding coastal and mangrove environment.

Ask about the Koh Sralao trip →
